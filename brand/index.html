Brand & Positioning

Brand strategy is where commercial ambition and creative clarity meet. I build brand as a business asset, defining positioning, messaging architecture and creative standards that hold across markets, channels and stages of growth. I have built brand strategy independently at each of my last three companies, often from a blank page.

By company

Smart Pension

Global fintech · UK pensions · Apr 2025 – Mar 2026

Brand & Retail Marketing Director

Joined a business with no defined brand strategy, operating in a highly regulated and politically sensitive category. Pensions as a category suffers from low consumer engagement and high complexity. The brand needed to work for both individual members and institutional employers.

  • Defined brand strategy, tone of voice and messaging architecture from scratch, creating clarity and differentiation across all touchpoints in a crowded regulated market
  • Positioned Smart Pension as an authoritative voice on workplace pensions and regulatory reform, building influence with government bodies and The Pensions Regulator
  • Developed a thought leadership programme that aligned communications to policy shifts and market changes, strengthening positioning with both consumer and institutional audiences
  • Engagement improved by 20%+ within three months of the new brand strategy being implemented

StashAway

Global fintech · Digital wealth · Feb 2022 – Mar 2025

Group Brand Marketing Director

Headhunted to build brand strategy and marketing from scratch across five international markets simultaneously. StashAway operated in a category where trust, credibility and clarity are existential to growth. The brand needed to challenge established financial institutions without the heritage or budget to match them.

  • Built brand positioning, messaging frameworks and visual and verbal identity independently, establishing a category-defining narrative that challenged financial services conventions
  • Drove top-three market position across five markets and doubled AUM within two years despite challenging market conditions
  • Restored brand trust and customer confidence following a period of significant market volatility through a sustained content and thought leadership programme
  • Maintained brand coherence and creative standards across five markets, managing agency rosters and internal teams simultaneously

Fintech · Insurtech · Jun 2020 – Feb 2022

  • Maintained brand equity and consumer confidence throughout the merger period, preventing churn during the highest-risk phase of integration
